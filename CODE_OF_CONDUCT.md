# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We welcome people who use, report issues in, and contribute to Redmine SLA. Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces. Assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and respect different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, threats, and publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer privately using the contact options on the [LikeHopper GitHub profile](https://github.com/likehopper). Include links and context where possible. Do not post sensitive personal information in a public issue. For conduct that violates GitHub's rules, you can also use GitHub's report feature.

The maintainer will review reports as privately as practical, may ask for more information, and may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern is not acceptable.
